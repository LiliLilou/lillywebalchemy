## Context

We Secure is a Belgrade-based cybersecurity company offering managed security, incident response and consulting to enterprises. They needed a site that feels as serious and modern as the work they do.

## My role

Freelance developer and QA with Jungle Creatives. Design by Jungle Creatives. Beyond the build, I helped shape the page structure, proposing sections and content ideas to the designer.

## The challenge

The site is rich in motion: a video hero, scroll-driven steps (Identify, Secure, Monitor, Respond), tabbed services, animated counters and a partner carousel. Keeping every one of those interactions working and readable across screen sizes was the hardest part.

## The solution

I built the interactions in Webflow and tested every page against my own QA checklists at seven breakpoints, adjusting layouts and interactions until each one held up:

| Device | Breakpoint |
| --- | --- |
| Large desktop | > 1920px |
| Desktop / laptop | 1440px |
| Smaller laptop | 1280px |
| Tablet, landscape | 1024px |
| Tablet, portrait | 768px |
| Larger mobile | 568px |
| Smaller mobile | 375px |

## Result

A live, interaction-heavy marketing site that stays usable on every device, from large desktop screens down to 375px phones.
